# Eastron meter

Compatible inverters for the instruction below.


Prerequisite

- **Zatobox one** or **Zatobox energy adapter**
- Ethernet cable cat5 or higher
- RJ45 connector and tool or premade cable [TBD zatobox shop] 


#### Step 1 - Connect meter to zatobox one

Connect rs485 connection of the eastron meter to the zatobox device. 

<img src="/img/zatoboxone/pinouttable.png" alt="" width="300"/>



#### Step 2 - Add to zatobox

Go to the device tab and push the plus button to add an extra device. Choose eastron meter single phase or three phase depending on the actual meter.
