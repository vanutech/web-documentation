# Solis Zatobox meter

Use zatobox energy adapters as meter for a solis hybrid inverter

Prerequisite


- **Zatobox Energy Adapter** or  **Zatobox One**
- **Solis meter connector**


#### Step 1 - Connect cable to inverter

Solis rai:

Use solis meter connector. Open the connetor and connect the A and B. 

Solis S6 new:

Crimp RJ45 connector with white orange as A and orange as B according to the Zatobox pinout. Only connect these two wires don't connect the other 6 to prevent bad signals.


#### Step 2 - Connect cable to Zatobox device

Connect cable to zatobox directly or through RJ45 splitter if other connections are also used.
